# Procedure 1: Faculty Check-In Template

**Audience:** HAAG Project Managers and Team Leads responsible for faculty sponsor relationships  
**Purpose:** Provide a consistent, repeatable structure for faculty check-in meetings to reduce communication gaps and ensure faculty expectations are documented.  
**Frequency:** Once per month per faculty sponsor, or after any major project milestone  
**Estimated Time:** 30–45 minutes per check-in meeting + 10 minutes to complete this form afterward

---

## When to Use This Template

Use this template whenever you conduct a scheduled or ad-hoc check-in with a faculty sponsor. Complete the form **within 24 hours** of the meeting while details are fresh. Store the completed form in the HAAG shared repository

---

## Pre-Meeting Checklist

Before the meeting, the PM/Team Lead should:

- [ ] Review the previous check-in notes (if any) from the shared repository
- [ ] Pull up the current project status or weekly reports from relevant team members
- [ ] Confirm the meeting time via Slack or email at least 48 hours in advance
- [ ] Prepare 2–3 specific updates or questions to bring to the meeting

## After the Meeting

1. Save this completed form to the shared repository.
2. Share a brief Slack summary (2–3 bullets) in the `#admin-faculty` channel within 24 hours.
3. Add action items to the master tracking sheet.
4. Flag any urgent concerns to the HAAG admin immediately.

---

## Integration into HAAG Structure

- This template is part of the **Faculty Engagement Framework** and is enforced through the monthly PM accountability check.
- HAAG admins will audit the shared repository at the end of each month to confirm check-ins were completed.
- Non-completion should be escalated to the relevant manager.
